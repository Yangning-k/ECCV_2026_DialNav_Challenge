# Data and model provenance

The base checkpoints, RAIN/RAINbow datasets, connectivity, features, and
tokenizer files are derived from the official RAINbow release:

https://github.com/happilee12/RAINbow

The Matterport3D-derived data is subject to the Matterport3D terms and the
official RAINbow dataset terms. This local release keeps the assets for
reproducibility; before public redistribution, verify the applicable terms and
replace restricted assets with the official download URLs if required.

The custom K80 weights are trained from the official RAIN training split and
are provided under the repository license.
